Battlemaster

Champion of the Physical Path

For generations, Battlemasters have been feared across the realm for their pitiless strength in war. Grueling training forges their bodies past what most mortals can bear, and they strike with crushing power and steady aim so the deed is finished in one blow. The title is earned only when a warrior proves worth against the fiercest foe—marking one ready to face any challenger on the field.

Tactical Retainers

Economy

Grants a 10% discount on the gold training cost when recruiting Infantry and Anti-Cavalry units.

Vanguard Press

Phase Manipulation

When the Battlemaster initiates a 1v1 PvP attack against an enemy commander sitting on a bordering city, the battle engine skips the Ranged Phase entirely after the first round, jumping straight to the Cavalry/Melee phases.

Iron Rally

Leadership & Cooperation

When participating in a Drop (Main vs Main stack), allied commanders attached to the Battlemaster's cell receive a +5% injury recovery rate for the next 30-minute tick.

Class abilities are not active in this alpha version. They are not permanent and are subject to change in future updates.

Voice of the Arcane Path

Rumors hold that Archmages first drew their power from an ancient relic sealed deep beneath the world's stone. That bond with magic is their only shield against those who hunt them to destruction. They are spellweavers honed in war and scholars who press ever toward mastery of their craft—and their rise has done much to kindle the age of sorcery across the realm.

Alchemical Logistics

Economy

Grants a 10% discount on the gold training cost when recruiting Artillery, Ranged, and Ranged-Cavalry hybrid units.

Arcane Volley

Border Defense Focus

When the Archmage is the defender in a 1v1 PvP battle (an enemy commander clicks on them from across the border to attack), the battle engine adds 1 additional round to the Ranged Phase before Cavalry or Melee calculations begin.

Leyline Anchor

Rapid Frontline Rebound

The Archmage can spend 1 turn to place a magical anchor in their current friendly city. At any point later, they can spend 1 turn to instantly teleport back to that anchor city from anywhere inside friendly territory.

Class abilities are not active in this alpha version. They are not permanent and are subject to change in future updates.
